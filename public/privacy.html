Anystring Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

Anystring is a guitar and bass tuner for Android, iOS, and the web.

Anystring has no user accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or backend server of its own. The app is designed to process the information it needs locally on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how Anystring handles information when you use the app.

Microphone

Anystring needs access to your microphone to detect the pitch of your instrument.

The app asks for microphone permission when you use the tuner. Audio from the microphone is analysed locally on your device in real time solely to estimate pitch.

Microphone audio is:

  • not recorded;
  • not saved;
  • not transmitted to us;
  • not transmitted to third parties;
  • not used for analytics, advertising, identification, or profiling.

Audio samples exist only temporarily while they are being processed and are discarded immediately after analysis.

Microphone access is released when tuning stops.

You can revoke microphone permission at any time through your device or browser settings. If microphone access is disabled, pitch detection will not work.

Data We Collect

None.

Anystring does not collect or transmit personal data.

In particular, Anystring does not collect:

  • names, email addresses, or other contact information;
  • account information;
  • microphone recordings;
  • device identifiers;
  • advertising identifiers;
  • location;
  • contacts;
  • usage analytics;
  • crash analytics;
  • browsing activity.

Anystring contains no advertising or tracking SDKs and does not attempt to identify you or your device.

Data Stored on Your Device

Anystring stores a small amount of information locally on your device so that the app can remember your preferences.

This may include:

  • custom tunings you create;
  • the last selected tuning;
  • tuning settings;
  • interface preferences.

This information stays on your device and is not synchronised or transmitted to us or to third parties.

You can remove locally stored information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. For the web version, you can remove it by clearing the site's browser data.

Because this information never reaches us, we cannot access, recover, modify, or remotely delete it.

Web Version

When you use the web version of Anystring, the hosting provider may process standard technical information necessary to deliver the website, such as your IP address and browser request information.

This processing is performed by the hosting provider under its own privacy policy.

Anystring does not use this information for analytics, advertising, tracking, or profiling, and microphone audio and locally stored tunings are not sent to the hosting provider by Anystring.

Children

Anystring is suitable for users of all ages.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children or from any other users. Microphone input is processed locally and is not recorded or transmitted.

Your Choices and Rights

You remain in control of information processed locally by Anystring.

You can:

  • revoke microphone access through your operating system or browser settings;
  • delete locally stored tunings and preferences by clearing the app's data;
  • delete browser-stored information by clearing site data for the web version.

Because Anystring does not receive or maintain personal data about its users, we generally do not have user records that can be accessed, corrected, exported, or deleted by us.

Where applicable, you may also have rights under data-protection laws, including the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal data and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

Changes to This Policy

If Anystring's functionality or data practices change, this Privacy Policy may be updated.

The current version will always be published on this page, and the date at the top will be updated when changes are made.

If a change materially affects how user data is handled, appropriate notice will be provided where required.
