Why Anystring

Most free tuners lock you into presets. Here every string is yours to edit.

  • Edit any string Tap a note, pick pitch and octave, save as My tunings.
  • Guitar and bass Including low F1 — large analysis window, on-device pitch detection.
  • Strings or Chromatic Target your tuning, or hear the nearest note live.

Your mic stays on your device. Audio is analysed in real time while you tune, never recorded, never uploaded. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
